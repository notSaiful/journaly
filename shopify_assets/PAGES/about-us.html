The 3 + 2 Calibrated Formula

Morning: 3 Minutes

Before checking WhatsApp or Instagram, anchor your attention with your morning chai:

  • Notice 3 small things already good
  • Pinpoint today's single most impactful focus
  • Set positive daily momentum

Evening: 2 Minutes

Right before turning off your bedside lamp, close the mental loops from the day:

  • Acknowledge one quiet win
  • Record a memorable moment
  • Release workday tension for restorative sleep
