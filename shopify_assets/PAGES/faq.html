The 5-Minute Daily Practice

How does the 5-minute daily habit work?

Each daily entry takes just 5 minutes: 3 minutes in the morning to set your intention and notice what is good, and 2 minutes in the evening to reflect on a small win and record what happened. There are no essays or blank-page pressure.

Why are the questions provided by IntelligentLab?

IntelligentLab specializes in behavioral habit formation. They designed these structured prompts specifically to make journaling frictionless. According to research, 9 out of 10 people successfully build and maintain a consistent journaling habit with this question framework.

What if I have never journaled before or find blank pages intimidating?

This journal is built specifically for you. You never have to wonder what to write. The questions guide you step-by-step, helping you capture your real life in just a few honest sentences.
