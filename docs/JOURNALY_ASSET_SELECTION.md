## 1. Asset Evaluation Framework

Every visual asset in `untitled folder 2`, brain uploads, and active repository is evaluated against 8 criteria (scored 1 to 10):
1. **Visual Quality (VQ):** Resolution, lighting fidelity, clarity, absence of AI artifacts.
2. **Product Accuracy (PA):** Correct cover text ("5 MINUTES JOURNALY" or approved editions), correct binding, authentic construction.
3. **Brand Fit (BF):** Matches modern memory & reflection positioning; warm paper, deep ink, and Journaly Yellow.
4. **Emotional Value (EV):** Evokes the desire to preserve memories, slow down, and establish a quiet daily habit.
5. **Ecommerce Usefulness (EU):** Answers "What am I buying?", builds purchase intent, eliminates purchase hesitation.
6. **Responsive Adaptability (RA):** Crops cleanly across desktop (16:9, 12-col), tablet (6-col), and mobile (4-col / portrait).
7. **Originality (OR):** Distinctive, bespoke feeling that stands apart from generic stationery templates.
8. **Trustworthiness (TR):** Feels genuine, credible, and premium; no fake anatomy or AI hallucinations.

### Classification Categories
- **A — HERO / SIGNATURE:** Top-tier assets that define key brand touchpoints.
- **B — STRONG SUPPORTING:** High-fidelity secondary assets for galleries, tabs, and product cards.
- **C — LIMITED USE:** Restricted to isolated thumbnails or modal accents.
- **D — REJECT:** Not used anywhere. Violates product accuracy, contains watermark/low-res artifacts, or appears generic.
